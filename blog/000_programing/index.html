How I Fell in Love with Coding

19 Oct, 2026

Definition
I may keep using the word between programming and developing interchangeably. I know there are some different exist but when I am going to use programming word; I want you you know, programming derive from thinking process to developing..

Developer develop after the research/thinking process has been done. It is not like this process has been followed in every scenario, but for simply joy which is qualitative thing, I won’t be able to explain and not the point of this section.

Love; What do I mean by love is probably something produce the dopamine while I do it. It is not like I don’t get tired or bored but I still want to do it; rational or irrational idk…

The Clay

What I can recall from my memory is hacking the system, to be precise, games. Even before class 8, I had bought a few used phones. But once I got some money from the Primary Academic Excellence Scholarship1, I bought my very first Android phone. BTW using a phone wasn’t considered good manners in my family. My sister had a phone, and yes, my father had a feature phone too. You know, the one with an antenna.

I loved playing games and always try to get more coins so that I could upgrade my cars, weapons etc. Theses are my high school memory. Later I found I liked hacking stuff, sniffing around, so that I could tweak the system the way I wanted. I had used a few custom ROMs, and game cheat engines, just a little, but that was enough to make me understand that if I could command, it would obey my will.

Also, I was a guy who could answer any technical questions about people’s phones. Any means whatever troubles the people around me had. It’s not like I knew everything from the very moment someone asked, but I had broken things enough times, which helped shape my understanding of the process and the steps needed to answer people’s questions.

Everything tech has been designed for humans to use, like derivation, every steps can be predicted the next stage and going into next step can verify my understanding. Often I could just go back & sometimes break :) This way I found my pattern to solve in many situations.

English became handy & mostly I learned English to understand technical/programming stuff. Also I used too much social media during high-school and college(8-12). Later I adoboded using social media and now I don’t use any doom scroll media. Will talk about Me and phone in a different section. Oh I loved reading pdf books… From my native » Bangla translated from English » English » English translation of foreign languages.

I like helping others2, inherited from faith and family, yea my father is too helpful towards others sometimes we(sibling’s) dislike his behavior, because he could focus on his work. But he is a good man, far better than me in many expects I can explain. Like why Dostoevsky wrote….. uhu not gonna say… Let’s focus on programming

I didn’t started programming yet. Also after 12th exam, I have gone completely offline after exam because I knew I have messed up academic & not possible to get CGPA 5/5) I did went offline but still had android phone just to read pdf, no call or social media. tldr: Surround yourself with good company, that only push towards your values, or live alone.

God of machine

If anything I like about education & university, the computer lab and Library. Those books in library are quite expensive to afford, even at my current stage. So I made sure make the best use of these two :) The liberians are also found of me ig.

Just seeing a matrix asci animation on terminal can drive me on programming; xd, But motivated na. During first semester, C the first love…books from library I read partially understood, search online for something. One day I found graphics header, dang… Let’s build some animation… During my first(or second, I forgot) semester in Computer fundamental course, Iqbal Sir said students can get extra marks by solving 100 from URI online judge (which is now beecrowd ). I did more, recursive function was still struggling thing at that point.

Later I discover other problem solution sites, keep solving it.. Why, I don’t know. I write some instruction, computer just follow it. If I write something wrong it slap me. It became easier by doing enough times, and I found my fun, the computer obeying me.

There are times to time, I did apply some math formula and produce results. It was fast, it was right and it was fun. I brought my first computer(Desktop) in my university life 2018(TODO: GET THE date) Intel i7 7700 with 8gb ram It cost quite fortune for a middle class family like mine. Oh yes, I came from village nearby city… Ney, not poor village but sufficient one.

I did play game, then thought why not make one. To make a game I needed 3d, started with c# and unity. My end desire was to build augmented reality stuff, because I liked to play with openCV(python). It was probably during 5/6 the semester. But back then I didn’t have GPU, so I couldn’t work properly. Built few 2d and FPP pew pew(shooting) games with just geometric shapes using pro-builders and then stopped focusing on game dev. If you don’t have the right tools, don’t go for it. Was it a wast? Nope.

I love everything

I wanted to become everything, or perhaps I should rephrase it with “I love making stuff”. I often gave up my vacations, touring/hanging out with friends or spending time with family. Just me and my computer that obey me, with some books from library. I split my day: morning for c/python for automation, afternoon for game and night for Android (java). I can’t exactly remember the order but I did split my time for each and everything that I wanted to do. & what wanted often was programing.

Between class gaps, I often spent time in the computer lab and library. A few of my mates joined me in the lab & we helped each other understand different subjects. Once we had to have an argument with the IT guy because we few were keeping him waiting, and he turned off the internet :) Yep, we went to the administrator & it was in our favor. The IT man never stood against us & we came to an understanding.

I even started attending Android (wireless) classes, which were 4–5 semesters ahead of my level. Thanks to Jangir sir, who became my supervisor. So yes, I became that advanced in programming. Often I learned online, and classes were just for attendance marks, so I later skipped some.

The Cost

We have so little time but so many things we want to do… friends, family, and the desire to learn. I ended up following my passion for playing with computers & programming. I ended up becoming a night owl, sleeping less, and turning into a zombie in class.

Sometimes teachers asked me to wash my face so that it would remove my sleepy mood. Later, sometimes I just asked teachers if I could take a 10–20 min nap in the middle of class. Once a teacher found out about my interest, it became no issue. I skipped classes for tech events like GDG, yap, sometimes at kinda faraway places… 3–4h journeys.

I was even okay with just accepting average grades, which I couldn’t care about because they weren’t CSE-related. I wasn’t that good at math, but I could program something to solve things for me.

I might have became ended up becoming arrogant. The computer club wanted me to join, but I skipped it because I found them less interested in programming and more interested in showing off themselves. I didn’t join any coding events… why, idk. I wasn’t interested in being in a crowd and showing off, or was I afraid of failure? Even during some university events, some companies wanted to hire me, but I still wanted to explore & learn… so I skipped.

programming, reading books & a little academics

COVID

Things were messed up, especially the economy. I had completed my first semester of COVID online. This meant I had more time for programming and learning new stuff. I had to drop the semester. For the first time, my parents said no when I asked for money (yeah, even for tuition fees). Before that, I had always gotten whatever I asked for. Yes, they trusted me and raised me that way. After some time, I was taken back to my home (village).

I had started Flutter before COVID. Since I was already doing Android development in Java, it came in handy. Now, I was focused only on OpenCV and Flutter. I got my first remote internship offer, with a stipend, at a social app for travelers. The app was released into production. I kinda felt the work was too small for me. What I mean by small is that the work only took 1–3 hours a day at most.

BTW, I was also working on OpenCV stuff at the same time. That’s when I discovered(actively engaged) Stack Overflow, where people ask questions. So, I just started answering them. Hell yeah, initially, it sucked. You would always find biased people, people proud of their so-called reputation who looked down on newcomers. Another key factor was regional bias :) Who cares? I just needed to help one person, the OP; if it solved the OP’s problem, that was enough. I found Stack Overflow more interesting than my job. While my work project was already released and I didn’t have much left to do or learn, I asked my PM if I could leave. They offered me 2–3x more(forgot exact rate), but I said that if they needed me, I would still be available, just not as an employee; like any other person on the internet. My PM had also seen me answering questions on Stack Overflow, where I had around 1–2k reputation at that point, I guess. Now I had all the time for bug hunting, answering questions, and building stuff that I liked.

Boom! Stack Overflow, answering questions…

Back to Academics

About a year after COVID, I enrolled in my academic program again. I had received quite a few good job offers, like $450/month from local companies and $45K/year from abroad. But I didn’t join any of them. I did attend the interviews, passed a few rounds, and then simply skipped the offers. No, dear, I wasn’t just testing myself. Well, that was true to some extent, but there was one major issue that made me turn down remote jobs: load shedding…. Hell yeah, 4–8 hours of load shedding a day! Continuing my academics while working remotely wouldn’t have been possible, so I had to decline those offers. However, over the next year, I got involved in a few projects for different companies from time to time, both local and foreign. Before my final year, I had only worked on projects for local organizations (including government organizations) and then handled bug fixes for a few others. Some people would just share code snippets they brought, bring existing projects that needed bug fixes or new features implemented on top of them. And yeah, most of those projects were from foreign clients and paid pretty well! I was doing but I wasn’t even very aware of what freelancing was at that point. XD

stack overflow

If you have dropped some semesters and the curriculum has changed, then you know what goes into it when you have to take courses with students from different semesters, with juniors, and no one knows you, while your other classmates have grown up together since the first semester… Yeah, I found a few old sections, which were called “old sections,” made for students with semester gaps.

So, one day, my teacher (who later became my supervisor) asked me what I had been doing besides academic. I said I liked answering questions on Stack Overflow. Later, I described what SO was. He knew my programming skills and had previously given me permission to take upper-level classes (Android) before. So he asked if I could get to #100 there. I said I could try. That semester, I only had 2–3 courses, and I just kept answering questions on Stack Overflow without focusing on anything else. It seems my supervisor had his eye on me… and one afternoon, he congratulated me on reaching #1. Ah, I checked then and found myself in 1st place… But in last moment I kinda became #2. BTW, I only answered Flutter questions, and I could tell exactly what was wrong just from the console.a

Freelancing

After graduation, I moved into the city (10 min by bus) and started accepting freelancing tasks… No, I didn’t focus on any platform. Once, a client asked me to join Upwork so that it would be easier for him. Beside I keep working for local as well. One interesting thing about me is that the places I have worked so far, multi-culture including foreign US and UK part-time jobs with good salaries, I had never applied to them… nor had I bargained over salaries. All I said to them was, just make sure that I understand the task. You guys handle the payment and all the stuff… I handle the code. There are a few experiments3 I have done while working with different cultures.

Job break

If you earn in dollars and live in a country like Bangladesh, you can earn 10–20x more. Besides I live minimal lifestyle with expensive hardaware xd. So even working part-time (20h/w) was more than enough for me, and besides, I kept maintaining a few production projects that required 2–3h/w at most. Fixing few bugs was enough to cover my monthly expense; I miss it while Ai handle it :)

The reasons I took a break:

  1. I had enough money.
  2. Want to become a one-man army who can build from idea to sale (system design, build, deploy, and maintain).
  3. Expand beyond just Flutter apps and do backend.
  4. The project didn’t align with my values.

About 4, you may ask why I joined it in the first place. Well, my finances got bad, the previous foreign project was lacking budget and project got suspended(became alive in 2026 again), I had free time, and I got scammed (from a experiment3), lost a few things… TL;DR: money+…

Yes, once they introduced the project I was going to work on, I said it didn’t align with my values. (Note: this wasn’t as bad as you may think, but I have quite strict principles. Even I don’t like working on scrolling social media sites where users do doomscrolling.) Then the CTO said to give it a try and offered 2–3x more than what we had signed in the contract. Nah… I said I was fine with the contracted amount; no need to increase it. Money wasn’t the issue, though I was working for it. My CTO split the tasks and assigned me only the ones where my principles wouldn’t conflict. Yet, during the work, I told him if he could look for other devs. So he did, and when they joined; after two months, I told my PM, I guess I can leave now. Then the job break started… and full-scale personal growth.
